# Privacy

This build is branded as **Usama**.

The source was reviewed for direct telemetry, webhook, analytics, or upload paths to the original project author. No such direct developer endpoint was identified.

Some features intentionally contact third-party services when you use them. These include configured AI/voice providers and public services such as Gemini, ElevenLabs, YouTube/Google, Steam, Ollama/LM Studio, and websites opened by browser-control features. Information sent to those services is subject to the service you choose and its settings.

The remote dashboard is intended for your own devices/network. Do not expose it publicly unless you understand and secure the network configuration.

Never commit API keys, passwords, tokens, or other private credentials to this repository.
